## What was broken and what I fixed

Your screenshot showed three separate bugs, all real:

1. **No planet selectable on the large map.** `LargeMap` planet roots were on
   layer `Default` (0); `PlanetSelectionManager` raycasts layer `Planets` (6),
   which only `SampleScene` planets were on. All 17 roots are now layer 6.
   Verified with `Physics2D.OverlapPoint`: 17/17 hit their own collider.
2. **Planet HUD did not follow zoom/pan.** The labels are world-space canvases
   on layer `UI`, drawn by the separate `UI Camera`, which sat at origin with a
   fixed size. Added `UiCameraFollower` to `UI Camera` in `GameCore`: it mirrors
   the main camera's position and orthographic size in `LateUpdate`. Both
   cameras now report the same size while zoomed.
3. **Planets overlapped.** `Outer NE/NW` sat at `y = 3` and `Outer SE/SW` at
   `y = -3`, 2 units from the Flank planets that need 2.56. Moved to `y = ±2`.
   Overlap check over all 17 planets: 0 overlaps.
